# Whisky in Hot Countries: proposed changes for the owner

This deck was remade from the older builder's package (`tmp/deck-packages/Whisky-in-Hot-Countries/`)
with its words kept as they were. Only mechanical fixes were made (listed at the end). Everything below
is a proposal and is **not** in the deck yet. Sources: the independent review
(`tmp/review/decks/Whisky-in-Hot-Countries.md`; "R B" for its blocking items, "R S" for should-fix,
"R minor" for its minor list, each with its number) and my own read of the script against the slides
and the outline ("found").

Each item: where, current text, proposed text, why.

## A. Facts and hedges

1. **Slide 3 (left card title). All Indian whisky as molasses.**
   Current: "Indian whisky" (rows: "Molasses from sugar cane", "Neutral spirit, some malt added").
   Proposed: "Most Indian whisky".
   Why: Module 1's trap: "Grain-based Indian whisky exists and is growing. Do not say all of it is molasses." The script already says "Most of what is sold in India" (R S6).

2. **Slide 3 (right card, second row). Pot stills.**
   Current: "One distillery, pot stills"
   Proposed: "One distillery" (with "in Scotch, pot stills too" said in the script), or "One distillery; in Scotch, pot stills".
   Why: pot stills are a Scotch rule; the EU definition the slide cites does not require them (R S7). Module 2 does not mention stills.

3. **Slide 5 (middle card). Company lore stated as fact.**
   Current: "Sold to shops and restaurants a few bottles at a time."
   Proposed: "Slowly, shop by shop, by the company's account."
   Why: Module 3's trap calls this "widely repeated and thinly documented. Present it as company lore." The script hedges; the slide does not (R S9).

4. **Slide 14 (Comparison row). Company claim stated as fact.**
   Current: "Four years is very young in Scotland. In Yilan the oak has done far more."
   Proposed: "Four years is very young in Scotland. Kavalan says a year in Yilan does the work of four or five."
   Why: Module 4 gives Ian Chang's "four or five" as a company estimate (R S10).

5. **Slide 15 (script). An age nobody has confirmed.**
   Current: "See whether you'd have guessed which one was three times older."
   Proposed: "See whether you can taste which one spent twelve years in Scotland."
   Why: no age for Kavalan Classic is in the outline, and Amrut's "about four years" is in "Not confirmed" (R S8).

6. **Slide 1 (script). The running order.**
   Current: "We'll go through them in the order they started selling malt, and taste each one when we get to it."
   Proposed: "We'll go India first, then Taiwan, and taste each one when we get to it."
   Why: Amrut sold in 2004, Kavalan in 2008, Paul John in 2012, but the deck goes Amrut, Paul John, Kavalan (R S5).

7. **Slide 11 (heading, script) and deck.json (Kavalan notes). One phrasing for the start date.**
   Current: slide heading "Kavalan: private distilling in Taiwan from 2002"; slide row "2005 ... builds a malt distillery"; script "It built a malt distillery in Yilan County ... in 2005"; deck.json notes "a distillery that began in 2006"; slide 13 note "a distillery that began in 2006".
   Proposed: slide row and script "breaks ground on a malt distillery" / "broke ground on a malt distillery" (Module 6's wording); keep 2006 for the first spirit everywhere else.
   Why: Module 6 says King Car "broke ground" in 2005 and the first new make was 11 March 2006; "built in 2005" and "began in 2006" read as two dates for one thing (R minor 17, found).

8. **Slide 2 (slide). Fifty years.**
   Current: "For its first fifty years Amrut made the kind most Indians buy."
   Proposed: "For its first fifty-odd years Amrut made the kind most Indians buy."
   Why: 1948 to 2004 is 56 years; the script says "fifty-odd" (found).

9. **Slide 2 (slide). Volume.**
   Current: "India drinks more whisky than any other country."
   Proposed: "By volume, India drinks more whisky than any other country."
   Why: Module 1: largest "by consumption volume", and its trap: "it does not lead on money spent" (found).

10. **Slide 2 (script), found.**
    Current: "several of the best-selling whisky brands anywhere are Indian brands you will probably never see in a shop here."
    Proposed: add a source (an IWSR or Drinks International brand ranking) to Module 1, or "some of the best-selling whisky brands anywhere, by volume, are Indian brands ...".
    Why: Module 1 does not establish brand rankings; Module 5 has only Original Choice's own claim.

11. **Slides 3, 7, 9, 10, 11 (script), found. Context not in the outline.**
    Current: slide 3 "India grows a great deal of cane, so molasses is cheap."; slide 7 "Bangalore is on a plateau and has a milder climate than most of India"; slide 9 "Goa is at sea level, hot all year and very humid"; slide 10 six-row barley is "the kind most Indian farmers grow"; slide 11 "Yilan is subtropical and humid, and the warehouses run hot for most of the year."
    Proposed: add a source for each to the relevant module, or soften ("Goa is on the coast and humid").
    Why: none is in the outline's "Nailed down" lists. Plausible, but the house rule is that every fact traces to the outline.

12. **Slide 9 (slide and script), found. How far from the coast.**
    Current, slide: "at Cuncolim near the south Goa coast." Script: "a few miles inland from the coast".
    Proposed: keep both, or "about 15 km from the coast" in the script.
    Why: Module 5 gives "about 15 km from Mobor beach" (about nine miles).

13. **Slide 16 (script), found.**
    Current: "Amrut, Paul John and Kavalan all follow it."
    Proposed: "Amrut, Paul John and Kavalan all call their whiskies single malt." Or add a source.
    Why: the outline does not record the distilleries' compliance with the Scotch and EU definition.

14. **Slide 14 (script). Stock structure.**
    Current: "None of our first three bottles tells you how old it is, for three reasons."
    Proposed: "None of our first three bottles tells you how old it is. There are reasons for that."
    Why: the review's only note on voice (R "checked and fine", last line). Optional.
